## Architecture Principles

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Principle                           Application
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **API-First**                       Reusable capabilities across
                                      products, interfaces, and
                                      integrations.

  **Modular by Default**              Clear domain boundaries that can
                                      evolve without unnecessary
                                      coupling.

  **Microservices When Justified**    Independent services when scale,
                                      resilience, ownership, or
                                      deployment boundaries warrant the
                                      complexity.

  **Event-Driven Where Useful**       Queues and events for asynchronous
                                      and cross-system workflows.

  **Secure by Design**                Identity, authorization,
                                      auditability, validation, and least
                                      privilege at architecture level.

  **Observable in Production**        Logs, metrics, health checks,
                                      traces, and operational visibility
                                      are built into delivery.

  **AI with Grounding & Control**     Retrieval, tools, structured
                                      outputs, permissions, and human
                                      oversight.

  **Built to Evolve**                 Systems support iteration without
                                      unnecessary rewrites.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
